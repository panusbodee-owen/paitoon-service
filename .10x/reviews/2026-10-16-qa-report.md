## Bugs found during verification (all fixed before commit)
| # | Severity | Bug | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | High | Restart (timeframe change/VPS reboot) reset regime to NEUTRAL → active grid cancelled + frozen | Regime state persisted in GVs; state machine advances once per bar |
| 2 | Medium | Failed basket start retried every 15 s → log spam | `AbortBasketStart` waits one bar |
| 3 | Medium | News window starting mid-bar left pendings live | Pendings cancelled as soon as the window opens |
| 4 | Low | `ticket = 0` would sit in recent buffer for 10 s | Guarded |
| 5 | Low | Live GVs could leak into tester runs | `RGGT.` prefix in tester |
| 6 | Low | Gap test asserted nothing (`>= 0`) | Asserts > 10 backstop closes and loss > X |
